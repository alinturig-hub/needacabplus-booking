# Plymouth supplementary roadworks

The October 2026 DfT permit archive seed contains 238 latest Plymouth snapshots whose published period had not expired on 9 November 2026. Source: https://opendata.manage-roadworks.service.gov.uk/permit/2026/10.zip . It does not cover November changes before the live subscription started. Archived snapshots are displayed as unverified and cannot trigger driver alerts. Reproduce with `scripts/backfill-plymouth-archive.py`.

`council-locations.json` contains approximate OpenStreetMap street positions for public Plymouth City Council notices, including approximate positions and, after a verified topology match, reported junctions or between-junction segments. A verified reported position does not confirm a live road condition. Ambiguous roads stay unmapped and remain searchable in the list. Coordinates can be regenerated with `scripts/seed-plymouth-council-locations.py` after fetching/parsing the council page. OpenStreetMap contributors: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Council notices: https://www.plymouth.gov.uk/roadworks . DfT data: Open Government Licence, https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/ .

Apply `scripts/migrations/20261109_street_manager_supplementary.sql` using the schema owner; grant the runtime role SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE on the three supplemental tables and SELECT on `street_manager_roadwork_events`. Enable runtime `STREET_MANAGER_COUNCIL_SYNC_ENABLED=true`. The authenticated internal job imports the bundled archive once and checks Council synchronization every 15 minutes and fetches successful publications every six hours (with a six-hour last-success guard). Failed or unexpectedly truncated publications preserve the last successful inventory. Records absent from a successful new publication are retained for provenance but removed from the current council list; disappearance does not prove a road reopened.

Live signed SNS events take precedence over matching archived events. Council reports are separate because they have no reliable shared permit identifier. The admin source filters expose this distinction and the last successful refresh. Council dates are calendar dates in Europe/London, never proof of a live closure. The archive remains excluded from automatic alerts. Council-only notices require the separately enabled driver-62 pilot, a fresh publication, a verified reported position, explicit current hours and successful comparison with Street Manager and TomTom. ROAD_CLOSURE_ALERTS_MODE remains observe until explicitly authorized otherwise.
